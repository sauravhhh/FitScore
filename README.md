# FitScore

Know your body. Own your fitness.

A single-page fitness calculator: enter your stats and get BMI, BMR, TDEE, daily calorie target, macros, ideal weight, waist-to-height ratio, estimated body fat and daily water needs — distilled into one clear FitScore (0–100).

Formulas: Mifflin-St Jeor (BMR), Robinson (ideal weight), US Navy (body fat estimate), WHO BMI ranges.

Live at https://sauravhhh.github.io/FitScore
