How your numbers are calculated

Transparent, peer-reviewed formulas. No black boxes.

BMR · Mifflin-St Jeor

The most accurate widely-used resting metabolic rate equation, adjusted for sex, weight, height and age.

TDEE · Activity multiplier

Your BMR scaled by activity level — from 1.2 (sedentary) to 1.9 (athlete) — to estimate total daily burn.

BMI · WHO ranges

Weight over height squared, classified against World Health Organization cut-offs from 18.5 to 30.

Body fat · US Navy

Estimated from waist and height using the US Navy circumference method when waist is provided.
